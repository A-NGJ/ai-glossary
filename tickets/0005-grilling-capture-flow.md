## Question

How does a term get *into* the glossary? Settle the propose-then-approve
pipeline: what triggers an agent to notice "the operator says X where I say Y"
(standing instruction? end-of-session sweep? explicit `/glossary add`?), where
proposals queue (inbox file? PR-like diff?), how the operator reviews and
approves (a review skill? manual edit?), and how rejections are remembered so
the same proposal doesn't recur. Depends on the entry shape and data home.
