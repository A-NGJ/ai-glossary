## Question

How is the tool packaged and installed? Explicitly *not* assumed to be a
Claude Code plugin — candidates include the `npx skills` installer, a plugin,
a cloned repo, or an npm package. Decide with the distribution research in
hand, weighing: install/update UX, ability to carry everything the chosen
loading strategy and capture flow need (skills, hooks, scripts), and keeping
the door open to non-Claude-Code harnesses later without designing for them
now.
